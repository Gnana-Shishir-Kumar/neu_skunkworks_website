§ 04.C

STANDING FORMATS

What we run on rotation.

FORMAT 01

Hackathons

Two major hackathons per year plus smaller internal jams each semester. Sometimes with sponsors, sometimes purely internal.

FORMAT 02

Speaker Series

Monthly talks from industry researchers, faculty, and frontier-lab alumni. Open to the broader Northeastern community.

FORMAT 03

Open-Mic Nights

Five-minute lightning talks on whatever a skunk is currently obsessed with. Low stakes, high yield.

FORMAT 04

Workshops

Hands-on technical workshops led by division members or invited mentors. Topics rotate with the divisions' current focus.

FORMAT 05

Reading Groups

Each division runs a weekly paper-reading session. Open to drop-ins. Schedule lives in Discord.

FORMAT 06

Partner Collabs

Joint events with the MA AI Hub, Boston Tech Week, Humanitarians AI, and other regional partners. Format varies.
