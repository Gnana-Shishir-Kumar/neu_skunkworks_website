2019 Unit Established 4 entries
MILESTONE

Unit founded.

AI Skunkworks established at Northeastern University by Prof. Nik Bear Brown as a student-led research initiative. Mission: radical innovation through small teams, short loops, and real stakes — borrowing the operating model from Lockheed's Skunk Works.

FOUNDER

Prof. Nik Bear Brown

Faculty Founder · College of Engineering

Founded the unit. Advised the first cohort. Set the operating culture that still runs today. Active mentor for current divisions.

COHORT

The 2019 skunks

Inaugural Members

First cohort spanning Information Design, Engineering Management, Data Mining, and Information Systems. Shipped the original Hues & Tones project to RISE finalist standing. Several alumni from this cohort now mentor active members.

PROJECT

Hues & Tones — RISE 2019 finalist

Flagship project

Designer-developer bridge project. Built by an interdisciplinary team that proved the unit's thesis: small teams across disciplines beat homogeneous departments on novel problems.
