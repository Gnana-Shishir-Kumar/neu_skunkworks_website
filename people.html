the crew

Founded 2019. Quiet 2022–2024. Revived January 6, 2026. Below: the cohort timeline, the current command structure, our institutional partners, and how to reach us.

§ 03.A — Year archives

Cohort timeline

AI Skunkworks launched at Northeastern as a student-led collective for experimental ML work outside the formal curriculum.

Operations paused while founders graduated and projects went dormant. The name and mission persisted informally.

Officially revived January 6, 2026. New command structure, new projects, roster filling in as crews spin up.

§ 03.B — Origin & mission

Why we exist

AI Skunkworks is Northeastern's student-run lab for building AI systems that don't fit neatly into coursework — agents, robotics stacks, research spikes, and hardware hacks that need a crew, not a syllabus.

We were founded in 2019, went quiet through 2022–2024, and came back in January 2026 with faculty backing and a mandate to ship again. The mission hasn't changed: give ambitious students a place to build real things, fast, with peers who care about the work more than the credit.

§ 03.C — Operating principle

How we operate

Field doctrine

  • Ship over theorize — working prototypes beat slide decks.
  • Document failures — the postmortem is part of the deliverable.
  • No gatekeeping — if you carry a project, you're crew.
  • Build in public within the unit — share context, not secrets.
  • Faculty advises; students command — Prof. Nik sets direction, students run ops.
